# GoFo Tracking Guide

Free guides to track your GoFo Express packages online.

## How to Track a GoFo Express Package

1. Find your tracking number in the shipping confirmation email or marketplace order page (Temu, Shein and TikTok Shop orders often ship via GoFo).
2. Enter it on the official GoFo tracking portal.
3. See real-time status: label created, in transit, out for delivery, delivered.

## Common Tracking Statuses

- **Label Created**: The seller created the shipment; the package has not moved yet.
- **In Transit**: Your package is moving through GoFo's network.
- **Out for Delivery**: The courier has your package and will deliver today.
- **Delivered**: Your package has arrived.

## Tracking Not Updating?

Wait 24 to 48 hours. GoFo usually scans packages only after they reach a US regional hub, so a gap between "shipped" and the first scan is normal.
